Shipping questions

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery to mainland addresses
Three to five working days after dispatch, tracked
Express delivery before noon
Next working day for orders placed before 2 pm
Which carriers do you use?
  • National postal service for parcels under two kilograms
  • Courier partners for larger parcels and express orders
Can I change my address?

Yes, until the order is packed. Write to support with your order number.
