Task: WCAG 2.4.6 Headings and Labels. Decide whether this heading or form label describes its topic or purpose.

For a heading: does its text tell the reader what the following content is about ("followingContent"), and can it be told apart from the other headings on the page ("otherSameKindTexts")? For a label or legend: does it tell the user what to enter or choose ("controlType")? Answer "fail" for vague, generic, numbered-only or duplicated text that does not describe the content ("Section 2", "Info", "Details" used for several different sections, "Field 1"). Answer "pass" when the text is short but clear in context. WCAG does not require headings or labels to exist here, only that the ones present are descriptive.

Fields: kind (heading, label or legend), text, level (headings), followingContent (headings), controlType (labels), otherSameKindTexts, pageTitle.
